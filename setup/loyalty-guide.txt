=========================================================================
 CHAUKA LOYALTY SYSTEM — SETUP & COMPLETE GUIDE
=========================================================================
Phone-number based loyalty: guests earn points on delivered orders, redeem
them for free items or bill discounts, and the manager controls all the
rules. No app to install, no plastic cards — just a phone number.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IT IS (30-SECOND OVERVIEW)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every customer is identified by their 10-digit phone number.
- Points are earned when an order is DELIVERED (not when placed), so only
  completed orders count.
- Earning rate: 1 point per Rs.1 spent (before tier multiplier).
- Redemption options:
    a) FREE ITEM   — any menu item whose price fits your balance
                     (Rs.120 item = 120 points). This is the good deal.
    b) BILL DISCOUNT — spend 10% of the bill in points for 5% off.
                     (Deliberately 2x worse value than free items, so
                      guests are steered toward item redemptions.)
- Tiers multiply earnings: Silver x1, Gold x1.5, Platinum x2, based on
  lifetime spend.
- Extra bonuses: birthday bonus (200 pts) and a visit-streak bonus
  (every 5th visit = 50 pts).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW IT WORKS FOR EVERYONE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMER (QR self-ordering screen, /app/customer/)
  - A "Enter phone to earn points" field sits at the top of the menu.
  - New number  -> "Set up loyalty" form: name (optional), birthday
                   (optional), and a WhatsApp opt-in checkbox.
  - Existing    -> balance banner appears: tier, points, "approx Rs.X
                   off" value, and a "Get my points on WhatsApp" button.
  - The phone is remembered on the device (localStorage), so returning
    guests see their balance automatically.
  - When ordering: tap the gift button on the cart bar to redeem.
    Free items are added to the order as "Rewarded with points".
    The bill discount is a checkbox in the same sheet.
  - After placing an order the screen shows "You'll earn ~N pts".
  - When the kitchen marks the order delivered, points land instantly
    (live socket update) and a toast announces the new balance.

WAITER (waiter pad, /app/waiter/)
  - The basket/send modal has a "Loyalty" row: customer phone (optional).
  - "Look up" shows the customer's tier + balance, or flags a new
    customer. Points attach to the order automatically.
  - Leave it blank to skip loyalty for walk-in orders.

MANAGER (manager panel, /app/manager/ -> Loyalty tab)
  - RULES card: points-per-rupee, discount percentages, birthday bonus,
    streak bonus, and the tier table (min spend + multiplier each).
    Save applies instantly to all screens.
  - CUSTOMERS card: every profile sorted by points (name, phone, tier,
    points, lifetime spend, visits, WhatsApp opt-in badge, Message link).
  - LEDGER card: the full points activity feed (earn / birthday /
    streak / free-item / discount), newest first.
  - WHATSAPP card: opted-in count, "Copy numbers" for broadcasts, and a
    hint when WHATSAPP_NUMBER is not configured.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. POINTS & MONEY MATH (exact formulas)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
EARNING (on delivery):
    base  = floor( orderTotal / 100 x pointsPerHundred )  [e.g. 100 pts per Rs.100]
    earn  = floor( base x tierMultiplier )
    +birthday bonus (if their MM-DD birthday is today)

VISITS = TABLE SESSIONS (not orders):
    The waiter assigns a phone to a table ONCE (header chip / 3-dot menu).
    Every order from that table automatically earns points for that
    customer — no per-order phone entry. When the table is closed (bill
    paid), the customer's visit count increases by exactly 1 and the
    visit-streak bonus (+50 pts) is awarded on every Nth visit. Closing a
    table with a wrong phone or no orders does not count a visit.

    orderTotal = sum of item prices, EXCLUDING redeemed free items and
    their modifiers. Discounts on the bill do not reduce earning.

REDEEMING — FREE ITEM:
    cost = item price in rupees = points (Rs.120 item = 120 pts).
    Only items with cost <= current balance are offered.
    Deducted immediately at order placement (not on delivery).

REDEEMING — BILL DISCOUNT:
    spend    = min( balance, floor( billTotal x 0.10 ) )   [10% of bill]
    discount = floor( spend x 0.50 )                        [5% off bill]
    Example: Rs.538 bill, 857 pts -> spend 53 pts -> Rs.26 off.
    (Hint shown in the UI: "a discount costs 2x the points".)

BALANCE RULES:
    - Points never expire (currently).
    - Redemptions are checked against the balance server-side; if the
      balance is short, the order is rejected with a clear message.
    - An order can combine free items AND a discount in one go.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE APIs
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
PUBLIC (customer screen):
  GET  /api/loyalty/settings
       -> earning rules + whatsappNumber (null when unset)
  POST /api/loyalty/register
       body: { phone, name?, birthday?, whatsappOptIn? }
       -> 201 customer profile (or 400 without a valid phone)
  GET  /api/loyalty/status?phone=...
       -> { exists:false } or { exists:true, customer:{...} }

MANAGER:
  GET  /api/loyalty/customers   -> all profiles (public shape)
  GET  /api/loyalty/ledger?limit=50  -> activity feed (max 200)
  POST /api/loyalty/settings    -> update rules
       body: { pointsPerHundred, discountSpendPct, discountValuePct,
               birthdayBonus, streakEvery, streakBonus, tiers:[...] }

TABLE SESSIONS (loyalty phone per table):
  GET  /api/tables               -> open sessions
  GET  /api/tables/:table        -> one session (customer screen prefill)
  POST /api/tables/:table/assign   { phone, name? } -> attach phone to table
  POST /api/tables/:table/close    -> bill paid: clear phone, count the visit
  POST /api/tables/:table/clear    -> remove phone WITHOUT counting a visit
       (use when the wrong number was entered — /close would award a visit)
  Orders automatically pick up the table's phone — no per-order entry.

ORDER INTEGRATION (server handles this automatically):
  POST /api/orders/customer   (customer screen)
       extra body: { customerPhone, redeemItems:[{name,points}], useDiscount }
  POST /api/orders            (waiter pad)
       extra body: { customerPhone }
  Points are awarded by the server when an order becomes 'delivered'
  (guarded so an order can only ever be awarded once).
  Order response includes: loyaltyCustomer, grossTotal, paidTotal,
  discount, discountPoints.

REALTIME:
  socket 'loyalty_updated'  -> customer balance changed (customer screen)
  socket 'loyalty_settings_updated' -> rules changed (all screens)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DATA STORAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
File: data/customers.json   (git-ignored local data, same pattern as
                              orders/menu/kitchen)

Shape:
{
  "nextCustomerId": 2,
  "nextLedgerId": 10,
  "settings": { ...defaults below... },
  "customers": [
    {
      "id": 1,
      "phone": "9876543210",      // 10 digits, normalized
      "name": "Amit",
      "birthday": "08-16",        // MM-DD (YYYY-MM-DD is auto-converted)
      "whatsappOptIn": true,
      "points": 763,
      "totalSpent": 657,          // lifetime, redeemed items excluded
      "visits": 1,
      "tier": "silver",           // silver | gold | platinum
      "pointsEarned": 857,
      "pointsRedeemed": 94,
      "createdAt": "2026-08-16T17:00:00.000Z",
      "lastVisitAt": "2026-08-16T17:00:00.000Z"
    }
  ],
  "ledger": [
    { "id": 1, "customerId": 1, "phone": "...", "type": "earn",
      "points": 857, "orderId": 42, "description": "...",
      "createdAt": "..." }
  ]
}

Ledger types: earn | birthday | streak | redeem_item | redeem_discount
Ledger is capped at the 500 most recent entries.

DEFAULT SETTINGS (manager can change all of these):
  pointsPerHundred: 100      (100 pts per Rs.100)
  discountSpendPct: 0.1      (discount spends 10% of bill in points)
  discountValuePct: 0.5      (each point = Rs.0.50 off -> 5% total)
  birthdayBonus: 200
  streakEvery: 5
  streakBonus: 50
  tiers: Silver (min Rs.0, x1) / Gold (min Rs.5,000, x1.5) /
         Platinum (min Rs.15,000, x2)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHATSAPP (free path — no API, no fees)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The system uses plain wa.me deep links. Nothing is sent automatically.

SETUP:
  1. .env next to server.js:
       WHATSAPP_NUMBER=919876543210
     (digits only, country code first, no + or spaces. Unset = the
      WhatsApp features are hidden everywhere.)

WHAT IT ENABLES:
  - Customer screen: green "Get my points on WhatsApp" button in the
    balance banner -> opens a chat with the restaurant, balance
    pre-filled. Works from anywhere (WhatsApp is cloud-based).
  - Customer signup: "Send my points updates & offers on WhatsApp"
    opt-in checkbox. Stored on the profile.
  - Manager -> Loyalty -> WhatsApp card:
      * "X of Y customers opted in"
      * "Copy numbers" -> copies 91XXXXXXXXXX,... for pasting into
        WhatsApp Business app -> New broadcast (send offers manually)
      * Per-customer "Message" links (balance + offer pre-filled)

WHO CAN BE BROADCAST TO:
  Only customers who ticked the opt-in checkbox. This keeps your number
  safe from WhatsApp blocks and is the legitimate way to message.

PAID AUTOMATION (future option, not built):
  Auto-sending points messages on delivery, or one-click broadcasts,
  requires the WhatsApp Business API (~Rs.0.4-1.5 per message in
  India). The structure is ready for it but it is not wired up.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Points didn't land after delivery"
  - The order must be marked 'delivered' (status change, not just
    cooking/ready).
  - Each order can only be awarded once (loyaltyAwarded flag).
  - Check the order has a customerPhone (waiter left the field blank?).

"Birthday bonus didn't fire"
  - Birthday must be stored as MM-DD (the UI now converts YYYY-MM-DD
    automatically; older profiles may need re-entry).
  - Bonus only fires on a delivery that happens ON the birthday date.

"Redeem rejected / not enough points"
  - Balances are checked server-side. The order is rejected with the
    reason — reduce the free items or remove the discount.

"Can't redeem — no gift button on cart"
  - The gift button only appears once a customer is linked (they
    entered their phone and their account exists).

"Customer left the restaurant — can they check points?"
  - Not today. The customer screen only works on the restaurant's
    network. The manual path: customer messages the restaurant on
    WhatsApp (the button saves the chat on their phone) and staff
    replies from the manager's Loyalty tab.
  - A public balance lookup page is a planned future option.

"Where is the data?"
  - data/customers.json on the restaurant's machine. Back it up along
    with the other data files.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. LIMITATIONS & ROADMAP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Points never expire (no expiry rule yet).
- No per-visit spend limits, no birthday-party offers, no referral
  codes yet.
- No self-service balance check from home (see troubleshooting above).
- No automated WhatsApp sending (paid API option, not built).
- Waiter-placed orders: the phone field is manual — a typo creates a
  fresh profile. Re-lookup shows existing balances to catch this.
